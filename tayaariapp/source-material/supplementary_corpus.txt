The tropical rainforest ecosystem is characterized by extreme biodiversity and high precipitation. The continuous canopy leads to the development of complex, multi-layered forest canopies because the abundant rainfall supports continuous growth. Temperate forests, whereas, shed their leaves seasonally.

The distribution of coal and iron ore forms the backbone of heavy industry. The Chota Nagpur plateau comprises immense reserves of metallic minerals. Mining activities in this region lead to significant economic development due to the high concentration of resources. 

High population density in the Indo-Gangetic plains occurs due to the fertile alluvial soil and abundant water supply. This demographic concentration differs from the sparse settlement in the Thar desert.
