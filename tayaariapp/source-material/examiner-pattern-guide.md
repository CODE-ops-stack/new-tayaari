# Step 2e Examiner-Pattern Style Guide

This guide establishes the 4 canonical MCQ formats required for all geography question generation, specifically aligned with SSC-Stenographer and UPSC Tier conventions.

## 1. Direct Fact (Basic Tier)
- **Structure**: Straightforward question stem seeking a specific factual answer.
- **Options**: Four distinct, mutually exclusive choices.
- **Purpose**: Tests recall of fundamental geographic locations, terms, or straightforward concepts.
- **Example**: 
  *Which river is known as the 'Sorrow of Bihar'?*
  *(a) Kosi*
  *(b) Ganga*
  *(c) Gandak*
  *(d) Son*

## 2. Statement-based (Medium Tier)
- **Structure**: Presents multiple (usually 2-3) distinct statements about a topic.
- **Options**: Combinations of the statements (e.g., "1 only", "2 only", "Both 1 and 2", "Neither 1 nor 2").
- **Purpose**: Tests nuanced understanding and ability to identify partial truths or misconceptions.
- **Example**:
  *Consider the following statements about Laterite soils:*
  *1. They are formed under conditions of high temperature and heavy rainfall.*
  *2. They are rich in humus and highly fertile.*
  *Which of the statements given above is/are correct?*
  *(a) 1 only*
  *(b) 2 only*
  *(c) Both 1 and 2*
  *(d) Neither 1 nor 2*

## 3. Matching Pairs (Medium Tier)
- **Structure**: Two lists or columns (e.g., List I and List II) requiring the candidate to match items correctly.
- **Options**: Different combinations of the matches (e.g., A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4).
- **Purpose**: Efficiently tests multiple related facts or concepts simultaneously (e.g., dams and rivers, tribes and states).
- **Example**:
  *Match List I (Dam) with List II (River) and select the correct answer:*
  *List I (Dam)*        *List II (River)*
  *A. Hirakud*          *1. Mahanadi*
  *B. Bhakra Nangal*    *2. Sutlej*
  *C. Nagarjuna Sagar*  *3. Krishna*

## 4. Assertion-Reason (Advanced Tier)
- **Structure**: Consists of an Assertion (A) statement and a Reason (R) statement.
- **Options**:
  *(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.*
  *(b) Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.*
  *(c) A is true but R is false.*
  *(d) A is false but R is true.*
- **Purpose**: Tests deep conceptual understanding and causal relationships between geographic phenomena.
- **Example**:
  *Assertion (A): The Western Ghats receive more rainfall than the Eastern Ghats.*
  *Reason (R): The Western Ghats are taller and block the moisture-laden South-West Monsoon winds.*
