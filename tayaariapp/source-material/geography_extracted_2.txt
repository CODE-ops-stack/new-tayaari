Peninsula & Plateau
A peninsula is a landform that is
surrounded by water on three sides,
but connected to a large landmass on
one side.
A plateau is a flat or gently sloping
elevated landform that rises above the
surrounding area. It is often referred to
as tableland due to its flat top.
Plateaus can be formed due to volcanic
activity, tectonic movements, or erosion.
Peninsula
Plateau
